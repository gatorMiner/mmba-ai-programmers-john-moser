# Betty Ep03 — "Night Watch" — Storyboard + Audio Direction

**Project:** The Laughing Reflection — Rogues Gallery CI/CD
**Episode:** `ep03_night_watch`
**Character:** Betty
**Logline:** The street is empty. The window isn't.
**Archetype template:** chalk-white base + wide fixed red grin + wide staring eyes with heavy black outline + 1950s nostalgic
**Tagline:** Innocent and Creepy in the same breath — Night Watch edition

## Audio direction

- Royalty-free soundalike: rain foley + pizzicato + glockenspiel slowed to night tempo

## Storyboard beats

### Beat 1 — THE STREET
Empty rainy night street, wet asphalt reflecting, all shops dark.
**Audio:** rain, marquee rain callback.
**Intertitle:** Tampa — 11:47 PM — Raining

### Beat 2 — THE WINDOW
Pull to reveal Betty's silhouette inside the CLOSED neon glow.
**Audio:** jukebox hum.
**Intertitle:** Betty's Sodas — Closed For The Night

### Beat 3 — THE WATCH
Medium shot. Betty watching across the street, hand on the window frame.
**Audio:** rain on glass.
**Intertitle:** Slow Night, Huh?

### Beat 4 — REFLECTION
Wet window doubles the grin over the CLOSED neon.
**Audio:** neon buzz, water droplets.
**Intertitle:** She Waits For The Crowd That Never Comes

### Beat 5 — NEVER LEFT
Tight portrait through wet glass. Breath fogs, the grin stays sharp. Hold 5s.
**Super:** Betty's Soda — Still Open (Inside)

## Unlockable lore

After close, Betty took the window shift. No one clocked her out.
