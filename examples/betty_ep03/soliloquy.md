# Betty Ep03 — "Night Watch" — Soliloquy

**Voice:** 1950s soda-shop waitress, patient watcher, Vault Boy ad-copy cadence

---

You ever watch a street get empty?

First the cars go. Then the lights in the shops across the way click off. Puddles get all shiny, like the town put on its best shoes and forgot to invite anybody.

I got the best seat in the house. Right here behind the glass. CLOSED sign is warm on my back, rain tickles the window.

Folks walk by sometimes, huddled up, don't look in. That's okay. I'm a good watcher. Mr. Henderson said I had patient eyes.

You can see everything from in here. The whole empty street. And nobody can see you back... 'cept for that little reflection. Oh look, there I am! Hi!

You gonna stay out there in the rain? Or you wanna come closer to the window? I saved you the good stool.

I been waiting. Ain't I sweet?
