# Betty Ep02 — "Closing Time" — Storyboard + Audio Direction

**Project:** The Laughing Reflection — Rogues Gallery CI/CD
**Episode:** `ep02_closing_time`
**Character:** Betty
**Logline:** The shop closes. The grin doesn't.
**Archetype template:** chalk-white base + wide fixed red grin + wide staring eyes with heavy black outline + 1950s nostalgic
**Tagline:** Innocent and Creepy in the same breath

## Audio direction

- Royalty-free soundalike, NOT the Len Stevens master
- Light orchestral pizzicato + glockenspiel winding down to a single note, vinyl crackle, neon buzz

## Storyboard beats

### Beat 1 — LAST CALL
OPEN flips to CLOSED. Music winding down.
**Intertitle:** Closing Time! Everybody Out!

### Beat 2 — THE COUNT
Register drawer counted.
**Intertitle:** The Till Is Balanced. The Smile Is Not.

### Beat 3 — THE DARK
Lights out, one by one.
**Intertitle:** Lights Out!

### Beat 4 — THE LOCK
The key turns — from the inside.
**Intertitle:** Locked In!

### Beat 5 — CLOSE
Tight portrait in near-dark, only the red grin catching neon.
**Super:** Open Tomorrow? — Betty never left.

## Unlockable lore

Betty's shift never ended. The CLOSED sign was for everyone else.
