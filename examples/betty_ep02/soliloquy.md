# Betty Ep02 — "Closing Time" — Soliloquy

**Voice:** 1950s soda-shop waitress, sweet-adjacent, unsettlingly cheerful, Vault Boy ad-copy cadence
**Status:** APPROVED by Johnny — Ep02 Closing Time (per source doc)

---

Well, sugar, you know how to close a shop proper?

First you flip the sign. OPEN goes to CLOSED, and the street gets real quiet. Then you count the till — balanced to the penny, just how Mr. Henderson taught me. Lights out, one by one, till it's just you and the hum of the icebox.

Folks think closing time means you leave. Bless their hearts.

I learned you gotta lock it from the inside. Keeps the cold out. Keeps the smile in.

They said "Everybody out!" but nobody said I had to go. The pie's still warm under the glass. The jukebox is just resting. And this grin? Oh honey, this grin doesn't punch a time clock.

Open tomorrow? Well... I'm always here. Ain't I sweet?
